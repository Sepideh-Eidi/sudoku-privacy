Privacy Policy

Sudoku by Sepidar · Effective October 24, 2026

This policy explains how the Sudoku app (package sepidar.sudoku) handles your information. In short: the app does not collect, share or sell any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Data stored on your device

To let you continue a game and see your progress, the app saves the following only on your device: the game in progress, completed daily challenges, game statistics and your settings. This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Ratings

The app may ask you to rate it using the rating dialog provided by Google Play or the App Store. That dialog is run by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies; the app does not receive any information about you or your rating.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If the app's handling of data ever changes (for example, if advertising is added), this page will be updated and the effective date above will change before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent by opening an issue at github.com/Sepideh-Eidi/sudoku-privacy/issues.
